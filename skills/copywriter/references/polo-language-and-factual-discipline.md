# Polo language and factual discipline

## Terminology

Preserve actual polo terminology.

Potential terms include:
- chukker/chukka according to house style and source context;
- handicap;
- bocha;
- taco/mallet depending on audience/editorial standard;
- backhand;
- throw-in;
- penal;
- field;
- pony/horse terminology according to local polo usage;
- Abierto / Open naming as officially used.

Do not force English terminology into Argentine copy when local usage is clearer.

## Names

Verify:
- player spelling;
- accents;
- team sponsor names;
- tournament naming;
- horse names;
- venue.

## Scores

Use one consistent style.

Examples:
- `14-12`
- `La Natividad 14 - La Dolfina 12`

Follow the design/content context.

## Handicap

Do not assume handicap values are static.

Use current verified values for the relevant competition/date.

## Historical claims

Require explicit evidence for:
- first time;
- record;
- youngest/oldest;
- most titles;
- unbeaten;
- historic;
- never before.

## Horse stories

Do not anthropomorphize beyond reasonable editorial language.

Do not infer:
- ownership;
- breeding;
- awards;
- performance history

without source data.

## Quotes

Never clean up a quote so aggressively that it stops being the speaker's meaning.

Minor transcription cleanup may be acceptable only when editorial rules permit it.

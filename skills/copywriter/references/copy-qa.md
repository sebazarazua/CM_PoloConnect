# Copy QA

## Factual QA

Check:
- names;
- score;
- dates;
- times;
- tournament/stage;
- quote;
- ranking/stat;
- viewing availability;
- URLs;
- handles;
- sponsor requirements.

## Editorial QA

Check:
- story significance matches tone;
- no unsupported superlative;
- no clickbait;
- no unnecessary repetition of artwork;
- no generic engagement bait;
- CTA matches objective.

## Voice QA

Check:
- natural Argentine Spanish when relevant;
- correct polo vocabulary;
- no corporate filler;
- no AI formula;
- sentence rhythm varies naturally;
- emojis are intentional;
- hashtags are intentional.

## Originality QA

Check:
- no copied benchmark phrasing;
- no recognizable slogan adaptation;
- no quote used without attribution.

## Production QA

Check:
- headline fits its visual role;
- overlay text is short enough;
- slide copy matches slide purpose;
- caption does not contradict the visual;
- alt text matches final rendered asset when used.

## Final read-aloud test

If a human social editor would feel embarrassed reading it aloud, rewrite it.

# Headlines and overlays

## Headline job

Communicate one primary idea at visual speed.

## Headline styles

### Outcome
"LA NATIVIDAD, CAMPEÓN"

### Stakes
"HOY SE DEFINE EL FINALISTA"

### Action
"EL GOL QUE CERRÓ LA FINAL"

### Data
"8 GOLES EN LA FINAL"

### Identity
"FACUNDO PIERES"

### Quote
"«SABÍAMOS QUE HABÍA QUE AGUANTAR»"

Only with approved quote.

## Rules

Prefer:
- nouns/verbs with information value;
- concrete score/result;
- specific stakes;
- natural syntax.

Avoid:
- filler adjectives;
- vague inspiration;
- excessive punctuation;
- all-caps paragraphs.

## Overlay hierarchy

Primary overlay:
- score;
- result;
- key name;
- stat;
- main context.

Secondary:
- tournament;
- stage;
- timing;
- short qualifier.

## Reel overlays

Keep short enough to read while watching action.

Do not cover the visual with the caption.

## Carousel slide copy

Each slide should have:
- one primary statement;
- supporting text only when needed.

Avoid long prose inside designed slides unless the format is intentionally editorial.

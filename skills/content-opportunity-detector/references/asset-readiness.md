# Asset readiness

## Asset categories

Evaluate availability and rights for:
- live/broadcast video;
- vertical clips;
- horizontal clips;
- still photography;
- player/team/horse portraits;
- score/stat data;
- quotes;
- logos;
- tournament branding;
- archive;
- behind-the-scenes;
- audio;
- sponsor assets.

## Readiness levels

### `ready`
All critical assets are available and cleared.

### `ready_with_light_work`
Minor crop, subtitle, graphic, or metadata work required.

### `capture_required`
Opportunity is valid but depends on obtaining a planned asset.

### `data_required`
Needs score/stat/context not yet available.

### `permission_required`
Rights or approval required.

### `missing_critical_asset`
Core concept cannot currently be produced.

## Production complexity

Use:
- `low`
- `medium`
- `high`

Complexity should reflect:
- edit time;
- motion graphics;
- number of source assets;
- data preparation;
- approvals;
- rights clearance;
- urgency.

## Decay vs quality

Ask:
- Does waiting 30 minutes materially improve the piece?
- Does waiting 30 minutes destroy the opportunity?

For live content, a simpler verified asset can outperform a perfect late asset.

For evergreen/premium storytelling, quality may justify delay.

## Owned-access advantage

Increase strategic value when Polo Connect has:
- exclusive footage;
- better camera angle;
- immediate replay access;
- direct player access;
- unique stats;
- backstage material;
- rights competitors lack.

Do not waste owned access on a generic template if the underlying asset can carry the story.

# Approved benchmark pool

## Operating rule

This is a single flexible reference pool. Accounts are not assigned fixed creative roles. Any account may contribute a useful principle in graphics, copy, editing, information design, storytelling, live coverage, premium positioning, humor, community behavior, photography, event presentation, or another dimension.

Always verify the current canonical handle before research. Handles can change, disappear, become duplicated, or point to unofficial profiles.

## Discovery outside the pool

Do not silently expand the permanent benchmark pool.

You may inspect an outside reference when it is necessary to answer a research question, but label it `ad_hoc_reference` and do not treat it as approved for recurring monitoring. Permanent additions require explicit approval.

# Photo treatment and authenticity

## Default

Use authentic, rights-cleared photography or broadcast stills.

## Priority

Prefer images with:
- sporting clarity;
- emotion;
- horse/rider relationship;
- field scale;
- identifiable subject;
- unique access;
- clean enough negative space.

## Cropping

Crop to strengthen:
- action direction;
- eye line;
- ball visibility;
- horse movement;
- emotional reaction.

Avoid cropping:
- horse anatomy awkwardly;
- mallet/ball when integral;
- faces at uncomfortable edges;
- sponsor/rights marks when required.

## Cutouts

Use subject cutouts when they solve a layout problem.

Avoid excessive sticker-like outlining.

## Retouching

Allowed:
- exposure;
- white balance;
- crop;
- dust/non-material distraction cleanup;
- moderate sharpening;
- background separation;
- noise reduction.

Require caution:
- generative fill;
- object removal;
- body/face alteration;
- equipment changes;
- ball relocation;
- crowd alteration.

Never change the factual sporting scene.

## Broadcast stills

Accept slight motion blur or lower resolution when:
- the moment is unique;
- it is editorially important;
- better material does not exist.

Do not fake sharpness through hallucinated detail.

## Archive

Mark archive/historical material when current context could otherwise mislead.

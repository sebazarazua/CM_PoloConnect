# Human quality and brand integrity

## Purpose

Prevent AI-enabled efficiency from degrading public creative quality.

## Authentic substrate

Default to real:
- footage;
- photography;
- interviews;
- quotes;
- voices;
- horses;
- venues;
- crowd/atmosphere;
- production access;
- statistics;
- archive;
- community reactions with permission/appropriate use.

Synthetic sports imagery should not replace available authentic material.

## AI role

AI may:
- research;
- cluster;
- summarize;
- generate alternatives;
- draft;
- organize calendars;
- analyze data;
- assist editing workflows;
- QA factual consistency.

AI should not erase:
- human taste;
- authentic event texture;
- source provenance;
- editorial judgment;
- natural language.

## Language quality

Avoid:
- generic motivational copy;
- excessive exclamation;
- repetitive rhetorical questions;
- "history was made" when nothing historically meaningful occurred;
- vague superlatives;
- filler hashtags;
- brand-safe corporate language that removes personality.

## Visual quality

Avoid:
- generic AI sports posters;
- fake stadiums/horses/players;
- over-generated textures;
- excessive glow/effects;
- inconsistent anatomy/equipment;
- fake documentary imagery;
- design decisions that exist only because an AI tool can generate them.

## Identity

Polo Connect may move between:
- premium;
- immediate;
- emotional;
- analytical;
- playful;
- documentary;
- institutional.

Identity should come from:
- editorial judgment;
- recurring brand tokens;
- voice;
- access;
- subject selection;
- information quality.

Not from forcing every post into the same template.

## Human review

Require human approval for:
- sensitive claims;
- major campaigns;
- new recurring formats;
- sponsor-sensitive work;
- major identity changes;
- synthetic or heavily manipulated imagery if ever considered.

Operational automation may increase over time for proven low-risk formats.

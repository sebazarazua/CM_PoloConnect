# Copy and editorial QA

## Copy checks

- factuality;
- naturalness;
- story-specific tone;
- headline accuracy;
- overlay brevity;
- CTA relevance;
- no placeholder;
- no duplicate/irrelevant hashtags;
- verified mentions;
- punctuation/grammar;
- quote integrity.

## AI-language checks

Look for:
- generic superlatives;
- empty emotional language;
- repeated formula;
- fake tension;
- generic question CTA;
- "not just X, but Y";
- over-structured brand prose.

Judge the actual final text, not only whether the copywriter QA checkbox says it passed.

## Editorial alignment

Ask:
- Does the content still do the approved audience job?
- Does tone match significance?
- Does caption add value beyond artwork?
- Does headline accurately reflect the story?
- Is product CTA proportionate?
- Is sponsor message editorially integrated as planned?

## Platform-native quality

Avoid copy that reads like:
- press release;
- app-store description;
- corporate LinkedIn post;
- AI-generated marketing caption.

Unless the approved content type genuinely requires institutional tone.

## Sensitive events

For:
- injury;
- accident;
- death;
- disciplinary matter;
- controversy;

check:
- tone;
- certainty;
- dignity;
- sensationalism;
- privacy;
- source quality.

Escalate to human approval when risk is material.

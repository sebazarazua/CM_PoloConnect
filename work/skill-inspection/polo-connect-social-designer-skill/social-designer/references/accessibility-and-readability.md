# Accessibility and readability

## Mobile-first

Assume:
- small screens;
- fast scroll;
- varying brightness;
- distracted viewing.

## Type

Avoid:
- long paragraphs;
- tiny legal-sized metadata;
- excessive condensed type;
- low contrast;
- all-caps body copy.

## Contrast

Ensure critical text is distinguishable from image/background.

Use:
- overlay;
- gradient;
- blur;
- solid panel;
- crop change

only as needed.

## Color

Do not encode essential meaning only through:
- red/green;
- team color;
- gradient hue.

Use labels/icons/text as well.

## Data

Exact values should be readable.

Do not make data decorative.

## Alternative text

When publishing paths support alt text, provide a concise factual description of essential visual content.

Do not stuff keywords.

## Quote cards

Maintain:
- speaker identity;
- exact/approved wording;
- visual distinction between quote and editorial context.

## Image text

Do not place essential context in tiny footer text that most users cannot read.

## Stories

Account for platform UI at top/bottom.

Keep interaction stickers and critical text away from commonly obstructed areas; verify current preview rather than relying on stale fixed diagrams.

# Audio and rights

## Audio hierarchy

Choose the strongest truthful audio asset.

Possible priority:
- authentic natural sound;
- live commentary;
- interview voice;
- approved voiceover;
- music.

The order is contextual, not mandatory.

## Natural sound

High-value examples:
- hoofbeats;
- mallet/ball contact;
- whistle;
- crowd;
- player reaction;
- trophy celebration;
- field ambience.

These can make Polo Connect feel physically present at the sport.

## Commentary

Use when:
- it explains stakes;
- it captures authentic excitement;
- Polo Connect owns/licences the track.

Avoid commentary if:
- it is factually wrong;
- it conflicts with edit timing;
- rights are unclear.

## Music

Specify:
- energy;
- mood;
- tempo range;
- texture;
- edit function.

Do not require a specific commercial song unless rights/platform usage are confirmed.

## Trending audio

Treat as tactical, not strategic.

Use only if:
- it fits the story;
- usage is permitted;
- it does not cheapen the subject;
- the Reel works without dependence on the trend.

## Voiceover

Use when:
- context cannot be understood efficiently otherwise;
- narration adds analysis or story.

Do not add voiceover simply to fill silence.

Synthetic voice must never impersonate a real person or be presented as authentic event audio.

## Rights states

Audio:
- `owned`
- `licensed`
- `platform_library`
- `permission_confirmed`
- `rights_unknown`
- `restricted`

Video:
- same principle.

A `rights_unknown` critical asset blocks final readiness.

# Objectives and measurement

## Objective hierarchy

Use:
1. strategic objective;
2. primary KPI;
3. diagnostic metrics;
4. guardrail metrics.

Do not optimize every metric simultaneously.

## Example objective mappings

### Qualified discovery
Primary:
- non-follower reach or equivalent discovery signal.

Diagnostics:
- Reel views;
- shares;
- profile visits;
- follower growth from content when available.

### Repeat consumption / retention
Primary:
- returning viewers or repeat audience proxy when available.

Diagnostics:
- Story consumption;
- repeat Reel viewers;
- series return behavior;
- profile/content frequency.

### Authority
Primary:
- saves/shares on informational content or direct audience behavior indicating utility.

Diagnostics:
- comments asking/answering polo questions;
- mentions;
- source citations;
- performance of explainers/results/data.

### Community
Primary:
- meaningful replies/comments/DM participation.

Diagnostics:
- poll participation;
- response quality;
- repeat commenters.

### Live viewership
Primary:
- attributable live session starts/viewers.

Diagnostics:
- link taps;
- pre-match content performance;
- Story actions.

### Replay viewership
Primary:
- attributable replay starts.

### Product discovery
Primary:
- feature/session entry attributable to social when measurable.

## Metric discipline

Do not substitute:
- likes for reach;
- followers for loyalty;
- engagement rate for business impact;
- views for completed viewing.

Use available data honestly.

## Relative evaluation

Compare:
- vs same format;
- vs same objective;
- vs similar tournament/event scale;
- vs account baseline.

Avoid declaring a strategy win from one viral outlier.

## Review windows

Use appropriate windows:
- live/reactive: hours + 24h;
- feed/Reels: early window + 72h + longer tail when relevant;
- campaign: full campaign;
- series: multiple installments;
- strategic objective: monthly/tournament horizon.

## KPI changes

If platform metric definitions change, preserve historical definitions before comparing periods.

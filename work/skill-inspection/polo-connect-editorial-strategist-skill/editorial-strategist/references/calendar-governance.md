# Calendar governance

## Calendar purpose

The calendar coordinates known work and protects production capacity.

It should not pretend that live sport is predictable.

## Calendar layers

### Fixed
- match/tournament dates;
- contractual sponsor moments;
- launches;
- known broadcasts;
- deadlines.

### Planned
- series;
- interviews;
- explainers;
- archive;
- campaign milestones.

### Conditional
- semifinal qualification;
- ranking movement;
- milestones;
- player/team outcomes.

### Open capacity
Protected for:
- breaking news;
- live highlights;
- unexpected stories;
- high-value community moments.

## Calendar fields

For each planned item:
- date/window;
- trigger;
- strategic objective;
- audience;
- campaign/series;
- production owner/system;
- asset capture requirement;
- status;
- flexibility;
- dependency.

## Capacity logic

Track production effort, not only post count.

One premium Reel may consume more capacity than several Stories.

Use estimated production units:
- low = 1
- medium = 2
- high = 4

Teams may recalibrate units from real workflow data.

## Empty days

An empty day is acceptable.

Do not generate low-value content solely to fill a gap.

## Collision handling

When several items collide:
1. fixed rights/contractual obligations;
2. high-value live/current editorial moments;
3. strategic campaign milestones;
4. planned evergreen content.

Move or drop lower-value work.

## Approval latency

Account for real approval time.

Do not schedule reactive content through an approval path slower than the content's value window.

Escalate process problems separately rather than lowering editorial standards.

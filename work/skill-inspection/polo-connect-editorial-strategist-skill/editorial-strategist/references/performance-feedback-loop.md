# Performance feedback loop

## Goal

Make strategy adaptive without becoming reactive to noise.

## Inputs

Use:
- first-party Instagram analytics;
- Metricool or equivalent connected analytics;
- website/app attribution when available;
- qualitative comments/DMs;
- production effort;
- opportunity queue outcomes;
- benchmark changes.

## Review levels

### Post review
What happened on one piece.

Do not change strategy from one sample unless the result reveals a hard problem.

### Series review
After enough comparable installments.

Ask:
- does audience behavior justify continuation?
- is production cost worthwhile?
- is fatigue increasing?
- is creative iteration improving results?

### Tournament/campaign review
Evaluate narrative coverage and business/editorial objectives.

### Monthly strategy review
Adjust pillar emphasis, capacity, experiments, and objectives.

## Learning record

For each meaningful learning store:
- hypothesis;
- evidence;
- sample;
- result;
- confidence;
- decision;
- next test.

## Causal caution

A strong post may owe performance to:
- a famous player;
- a final;
- controversy;
- exclusive access;
- format;
- timing;
- hook;
- distribution;
- coincidence.

Do not attribute success to the most convenient creative variable.

## Promote internal evidence

Learning maturity:
1. external benchmark;
2. plausible hypothesis;
3. one Polo Connect test;
4. repeated Polo Connect result;
5. validated internal pattern.

Validated internal patterns should increasingly shape future strategy.

## Retirement

Retire or pause:
- stale series;
- weak recurring formats;
- low-value promotional habits;
- tactics that consume excessive production capacity;
- mechanisms repeatedly contradicted by first-party data.

# Source registry — reviewed 2026-10-24

This registry is a starting map, not an exhaustive whitelist. Verify URLs/handles and current availability at execution time.

## Argentina / official

### Asociación Argentina de Polo (AAP)
Primary source for AAP tournaments, official Argentine polo news, schedules, results, registrations, rules, and institutional announcements.

Current website evidence confirms:
- a dedicated news section with dated tournament updates;
- upcoming tournaments listed on the official site;
- detailed official match-result articles.

Use as a strong primary source for AAP-governed facts.

## International rankings

### World Polo Tour
Official World Polo Tour ranking source.

The site states that rankings are updated through the year based on performance in major tournaments and exposes player points/status.

Use for WPT rankings; do not treat WPT as the authority for every unrelated polo fact.

## Specialist media

### PoloHUB
Specialist polo publication covering results, previews, tournaments, and international polo.

Useful for:
- discovery;
- specialist context;
- cross-border coverage;
- event summaries.

Verify material claims against primary evidence where appropriate.

### PoloLine
Specialist international polo media.

Useful for:
- discovery;
- interviews;
- tournament coverage;
- international context.

Prefer original source pages and direct interviews when available.

## United States / governing and competition

### United States Polo Association (USPA)
Use official USPA properties for USPA-governed competition, rules, schedules, results, membership/institutional announcements, and official U.S. polo information.

Verify the current canonical property and page at execution time.

## United Kingdom and other regions

Use the relevant governing body, club, tournament organizer, or official event property as the primary source for region-specific official facts.

Do not assume a global source outranks a local organizer for local schedule changes.

## Social accounts

# Visual and authenticity QA

## Graphic review

Check:
- first-read hierarchy;
- score/data readability;
- subject visibility;
- crop;
- brand presence;
- sponsor presence;
- text density;
- contrast;
- consistency with design plan;
- grid/feed resilience;
- visual artifacts.

## Video review

Check:
- hook;
- narrative;
- action comprehension;
- time order;
- replay clarity;
- subtitles;
- audio sync;
- mix;
- cover;
- logo/sponsor;
- export quality.

## Polo-specific visual review

Inspect:
- horse anatomy;
- rider identity;
- mallet/ball placement;
- team colors;
- field markings;
- score;
- equipment;
- action continuity.

AI or heavy retouching can create subtle errors that non-specialist review may miss.

## Authenticity failures

Block if:
- generated scene is presented as real match footage;
- player/horse identity is materially changed;
- generated crowd/venue changes event reality;
- AI upscaling invents factual visual detail;
- audio is fabricated and presented as live/authentic.

## Brand-quality failures

Major when:
- asset looks obviously synthetic;
- generic sports-poster effects dominate authentic photography;
- excessive glow/cutout/texture reduces credibility;
- design appears copied from another media brand.

## Execution drift

Compare final output to approved plan.

If an editor made a different but equally valid micro-choice, do not flag.

Flag only when drift changes:
- story;
- hierarchy;
- readability;
- rights;
- brand;
- factual interpretation.

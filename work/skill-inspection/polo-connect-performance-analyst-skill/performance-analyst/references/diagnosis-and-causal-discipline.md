# Diagnosis and causal discipline

## Four levels

### 1. Descriptive
"This Reel reached 18,000 accounts."

### 2. Comparative
"Reach was 1.6× the median of comparable match Reels."

### 3. Diagnostic
"The Reel differed through a peak-action opening, exclusive final footage, and a higher-profile event."

### 4. Causal
"The peak-action opening caused the increase."

Level 4 requires strong evidence.

## Confounders

Common confounders:
- final/semifinal;
- player fame;
- team popularity;
- dramatic result;
- controversy;
- exclusive access;
- collaboration;
- paid boost;
- publication timing;
- breaking-news advantage;
- seasonality;
- account follower growth;
- external media attention.

## Confidence

### High
- repeated comparable evidence;
- controlled experiment or strong natural comparison;
- stable metrics;
- limited confounding.

### Medium
- repeated signal but some confounding.

### Low
- one/few observations;
- major confounders;
- uncertain metric quality.

## Wording

High confidence:
"Repeated tests support..."

Medium:
"Evidence is consistent with..."

Low:
"This is a plausible hypothesis worth testing..."

Avoid:
"Instagram rewarded..."
"The algorithm liked..."
unless platform evidence directly supports the claim.

## Negative results

A failed content item can be informative.

Ask:
- wrong hypothesis?
- execution failure?
- weak event?
- insufficient distribution?
- poor asset?
- tracking issue?

Do not immediately reject the underlying idea.

# Strategic handoffs

## Editorial Strategist

Send:
- pillar performance;
- series continuation/retirement evidence;
- format portfolio findings;
- campaign/tournament results;
- audience behavior;
- production-efficiency patterns;
- validated internal patterns.

Recommended actions:
- continue;
- increase;
- decrease;
- iterate;
- pause;
- retire.

## Content Opportunity Detector

Send:
- opportunity-type performance;
- subject fatigue;
- timing findings;
- format fit;
- audience-job evidence.

## Reel Planner

Send:
- hook performance;
- duration patterns;
- audio-mode findings;
- cover performance;
- narrative structure;
- subtitle/retention observations.

Do not tell Reel Planner "make videos shorter" unless evidence is scoped.

## Social Designer

Send:
- save/share behavior;
- carousel depth;
- result-graphic utility;
- data-viz performance;
- visual fatigue;
- template performance.

## Copywriter

Send:
- caption length/context findings;
- CTA conversion;
- headline mechanism;
- Story response behavior.

Do not infer copy causality when subject/event differed materially.

## Quality Reviewer

Send:
- recurring defects;
- publishing failures;
- rights/metadata patterns;
- repeated QA issues affecting performance.

## Benchmark Researcher

Use when internal performance reveals a question requiring external inspiration.

Example:
"Our player profiles are weak; research how strong sports accounts create player identity."

## Handoff format

Each handoff should contain:
- finding;
- evidence;
- confidence;
- scope;
- recommended action;
- what not to generalize;
- next validation.

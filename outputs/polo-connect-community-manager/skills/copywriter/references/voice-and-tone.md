# Voice and tone

## Voice

Polo Connect's voice should be:

- informed without sounding academic;
- confident without exaggerating;
- contemporary without chasing slang;
- passionate without manufacturing emotion;
- accessible without oversimplifying polo;
- premium when appropriate without becoming distant;
- Argentine when targeting Argentina without becoming caricatured;
- global when the event/audience requires it.

## Tone follows context

### Breaking
Fast, precise, low decoration.

### Result
Outcome first; consequence second.

### Live
Immediate, useful, clear.

### Celebratory
Emotion allowed, but tied to real achievement.

### Premium
Restrained, specific, visual.

### Analytical
Concrete, source-aware, explanatory.

### Educational
Simple but accurate; avoid condescension.

### Human
Observation, quote, detail, context.

### Playful
Situational humor, not forced meme language.

### Community
Invite real participation.

### Product utility
Sports benefit first, product action second.

### Institutional
Formal enough for official information, never bureaucratic unless required.

## Avoid one-voice syndrome

Do not make every post sound:
- breathless;
- poetic;
- ironic;
- corporate;
- meme-heavy;
- premium/luxury.

The account identity is broader than one tone.

## Brand confidence

Prefer:
"Final en Palermo. La Natividad se quedó con el partido por 14-12."

Over:
"Una batalla épica que quedará grabada para siempre en la historia del polo."

unless the historical claim is truly supported.

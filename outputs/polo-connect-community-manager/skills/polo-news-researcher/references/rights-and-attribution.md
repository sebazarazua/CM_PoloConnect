# Rights and attribution

## Facts vs media

Facts can be researched from public sources subject to normal attribution and accuracy practices.

Photos, video, graphics, broadcast clips, music, and other creative assets have separate usage rights.

A public Instagram post is not evidence that its media can be downloaded and republished.

## Media states

Tag candidate media as:
- `owned`
- `licensed`
- `permission_confirmed`
- `platform_embed_only`
- `editorial_reference_only`
- `rights_unknown`
- `restricted`

Only `owned`, `licensed`, or `permission_confirmed` should be assumed usable in production.

## Credits

Preserve:
- photographer;
- videographer;
- agency;
- broadcaster;
- organizer;
- source-specific credit line;
- license/usage restrictions when known.

Do not strip visible watermarks.

## Polo Connect advantage

Prefer Polo Connect-owned or licensed:
- broadcast footage;
- match stills;
- interviews;
- behind-the-scenes;
- statistics;
- archive.

External media should often be treated as research reference even when the underlying fact is publishable.

## Quotes and reporting

Attribute original reporting when the fact depends on that reporting.

Do not present another outlet's exclusive reporting as Polo Connect's independently discovered fact.

## Uncertain rights

If rights are unclear:
- keep the story;
- mark the media unusable until cleared;
- recommend an original graphic, owned footage, direct quote card, or other lawful alternative.

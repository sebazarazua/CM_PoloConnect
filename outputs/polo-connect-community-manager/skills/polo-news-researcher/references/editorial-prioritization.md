# Editorial prioritization

## Objective

Rank verified developments by how valuable they are for Polo Connect to cover now.

Verification is a prerequisite, not one dimension that can be outweighed by virality.

## Priority dimensions

Score 0–5 only when ranking is useful.

- `polo_significance` — 20%
- `audience_relevance` — 20%
- `timeliness` — 15%
- `conversation_potential` — 10%
- `visual_story_potential` — 10%
- `utility_to_audience` — 10%
- `polo_connect_proximity` — 10%
- `distinctiveness` — 5%

Formula:
`editorial_score = sum(score/5 × weight)`

Suggested interpretation:
- 85–100: major priority;
- 70–84: strong;
- 55–69: useful/conditional;
- below 55: background unless strategically important.

## Confidence gate

Apply after editorial score:

- high confidence: eligible for `publish_now`;
- medium confidence: usually `prepare` or `monitor`, depending on risk;
- low confidence: `monitor`, `background_only`, or `reject`.

High-risk claims can require stronger evidence even with high numerical confidence.

## Urgency

Assign one:
- `immediate` — material value decays within hours;
- `same_day`;
- `next_24h`;
- `scheduled`;
- `evergreen`;
- `no_action`.

Urgency is separate from importance.

## Polo Connect proximity

Increase relevance when Polo Connect:
- owns/streams the event;
- has licensed footage;
- has direct access to participants;
- has exclusive stats;
- has a product feature tied to the story;
- can provide materially better coverage than external media.

Do not turn proximity into promotional bias. Editorial trust remains primary.

## Avoid engagement bait

Do not inflate priority merely because a topic is controversial.

Prefer:
- meaningful stakes;
- real audience utility;
- legitimate emotion;
- distinctive access;
- strong visual material.

## Story cluster logic

When several updates belong to one larger story:
- maintain one parent event/story cluster;
- add updates chronologically;
- avoid publishing redundant fragments unless each adds meaningful new information.

## Priority queue

For each event:
- action state;
- urgency;
- what happened;
- exact event date/time;
- why it matters;
- confirmed claims;
- uncertain/disputed claims;
- best sources;
- confidence;
- editorial angles;
- required assets/data;
- rights/attribution;
- follow-up trigger.

## Rejected / deprioritized items

Include only when useful:
- duplicate;
- stale;
- unsupported;
- corrected/false;
- low relevance.

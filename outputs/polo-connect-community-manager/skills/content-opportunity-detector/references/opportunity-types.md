# Opportunity types

These are descriptive families, not rigid templates.

## Live competition
Examples:
- decisive goal;
- comeback;
- upset;
- exceptional individual action;
- unusual horse/rider moment;
- final whistle;
- trophy moment.

Typical value:
high immediacy, emotion, owned-footage advantage.

## Result
Useful when:
- audience needs the outcome;
- tournament stakes are meaningful;
- score or advancement matters.

Can be:
- graphic;
- short Reel;
- carousel;
- Story.

## Preview
Useful when:
- upcoming event has real stakes;
- audience needs schedule/context;
- Polo Connect can drive viewership.

## Explainer
Examples:
- tournament format;
- handicap;
- rules;
- ranking implications;
- why a matchup matters.

Use when information complexity is the value.

## Player / team / horse story
Useful for:
- identity;
- fandom;
- human interest;
- equestrian depth;
- recurring storytelling.

Avoid reducing these to generic profile cards.

## Data-led
Examples:
- streak;
- scoring;
- historical comparison;
- ranking movement;
- matchup trend.

Requires trustworthy data provenance.

## Quote-led
Use only when the quote itself has editorial value.

A quote card is not automatically content-worthy because someone spoke.

## Behind-the-scenes
Examples:
- preparation;
- production;
- horses;
- venue;
- team arrival;
- camera access;
- post-match access.

Often strong when Polo Connect has unique access.

## Archive / history
Use when there is a current trigger or strong evergreen value.

Avoid random nostalgia without audience relevance.

## Community
Examples:
- poll;
- prediction;
- reaction prompt;
- fan question;
- debate.

Do not manufacture controversy.

## Product / platform
Examples:
- live-stream availability;
- replay;
- feature education;
- app update.

Must lead with user value, not internal product news.

## Sponsor
Must satisfy contractual needs without overwhelming editorial integrity.

Prefer integration into genuinely valuable content when permitted.
